The luma check answers does one frame decode. This answers does it play — the question that decides whether a resolution rung is a capability or a curiosity.

It measures the whole per-frame cost, not just decode. The globe uploads every video frame to a WebGL texture, so a device can decode an 8192×4096 stream comfortably and still be sunk by texImage2D. Both are timed separately here.
